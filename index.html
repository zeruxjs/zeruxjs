A COMMON FOUNDATION

Your ambition is big.
Your stack can stay connected.

A framework with a clear goal: make the parts of an application easier to understand and build together.

01

One request. Connected services.

Move from a route to application services without losing the thread. The runtime brings configuration, middleware, plugins and lifecycle management together.

Understand the runtime ↗
02

Your stack. Your building blocks.

Use the framework, or start with a focused package. Choose adapters for databases, cache, files and email around the needs of your application.

Explore the ecosystem ↗
03

TypeScript, all the way through.

Work with typed request contexts, configuration and public APIs. Follow source-linked guides that explain setup, ownership and the boundaries of each feature.
